How it works

Two open models, one job each.

01

You describe the item

Category, materials, size, make-time, condition, photos, season. The same facts you'd put in a listing anyway.

02

TabPFN-v2 predicts the price

An open-weight tabular foundation model predicts the sell price from past sold listings in one CPU pass, with a 10–90% range and a measured error, not a guess.

03

Gemma writes the listing

A local open-weight Gemma 3 model turns the item and that exact price into a title, description, and tags, grounded on the number, never inventing one.

Why open innovation matters

The open pieces are what make the business work.

Your sales data stays yours. Your margins and what sells are a seller's edge. PricePilot runs on your laptop, so nothing is uploaded to a competitor's cloud or logged to a server you don't own.

$0 per listing. Open weights mean no per-call API fee. A seller pricing 300 items a month pays nothing. A closed API turns pricing into a recurring bill.

Tune it to your niche. Swap in your own exported sales CSV and the model learns your corner of the market (vintage synths, hand-poured candles) where a general API is weak.

No vendor lock-in. Point the listing writer at any open-weight model Ollama runs, or retrain the predictor. You own the stack, not someone else's pricing page.
